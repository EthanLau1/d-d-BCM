Why BCM Exists: Concept & Purpose

01

Artistic Rebirth of Waste (BottleCap → Motorcycle)

Sparked by a visceral shock at a waste recycling depot: towering mounds of discarded plastic bottle caps awaiting landfill or incineration. BCM (BottleCap Motorcycle) washes, sorts, and deconstructs everyday drink caps, reimagining them as 109 distinct mechanical wheels, giving the humblest waste a second life in hard-edged industrial art.

02

Two Generations, One Craft Obsession

The street aesthetics of a twenty-something designer fused with the engineering rigour of a thirty-something tech builder. Pouring heart and soul into 101 bespoke chassis designs. "Combining sustainability with original motorbike craft to create products of genuine substance" remains our singular founding conviction.

03

No Rhetoric, Just Honest Handcraft

Strictly honouring 5R zero-waste principles: 100% reclaimed materials, zero virgin plastics. Having hosted numerous hands-on workshops in Guangzhou, guiding over a hundred makers to handcraft tactile bike toys, making sustainable living an immediate, accessible reality.

🎨 Original Design & CC0 Dedication Statement

BCM = BottleCap Motorcycle: An artistic synergy of discarded bottle caps and mechanical motorbikes. Each complete BCM digital motorcycle = Bespoke Chassis & Mechanical Engineering (Original Motorcycle) + Everyday Beverage Bottle Cap Elements (Discarded BottleCaps).

Chassis and mechanical architecture are 100% our original design: Comprising 101 bespoke frames, skeletal structures, wheel hubs, engines, handlebars, and suspension systems, conceived and illustrated entirely in-house. These are our authentic artisan creations, over which we retain full intellectual provenance.

All 7,700 Digital Motorbike NFTs are dedicated to CC0: Precisely because the vehicles are our original designs, we proactively dedicate all 7,700 digital motorbike NFTs to the global community under CC0 1.0 Universal (No Rights Reserved). Anyone is free to remix, modify, commercialise, or produce derivative works without requiring permission.

Bottle cap elements originate from existing beverage brands (Copyright not owned by BCM): Cap textures incorporate logos of commercially available beverage brands (colas, mineral waters, teas, fruit juices). These logos and insignia remain the registered trademarks and intellectual property of their respective owners and are not owned by BCM.

⚠️ Pop Art Fair-Dealing & Non-Endorsement Notice: BCM adopts a Pop Art collage approach as a homage to street custom culture and environmental contemplation. BCM maintains no commercial sponsorship, official partnership, or endorsement from any featured beverage brand.

07 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About BCM

What does the name BCM stand for?
BCM stands for BottleCap Motorcycle.
It springs from the clash and reconstruction of two worlds: on one hand, the humblest, everyday disposable item — BottleCap (discarded plastic drink caps); on the other, the industrial aesthetics of freedom and raw horsepower — the Motorcycle.
Through 101 bespoke chassis designs, the team transforms discarded caps into mechanical wheels, ensuring overlooked waste is seen anew as striking contemporary art and digital collectibles.
What are BCM Heroes, and what is their relationship to BCM Motorbikes?
BCM Heroes are pure community environmental champions, not riders for the bikes.
Our core aim in initiating BCM Hero co-creation is exploring "what Earth-defending eco-champions look like in the eyes of diverse creators — especially children and grassroots makers."

· BCM Motorbikes: Symbolising low-carbon mobility and green transit, embodied by 101 bespoke chassis and CC0 on-chain collectibles.
· BCM Heroes: An independent, community-driven eco-character universe. Anyone can define their own champion through "Sketch ➔ Clay Moulding ➔ Assign Eco Superpower" to receive an archival registry code. You do not need to hold an NFT — participation is 100% barrier-free!
How are the motorbikes released? (What is the Daily Mint?)
One Machine. Every Day. Forever.
The smart contract automatically generates and releases 1 bespoke motorbike every 24 hours for public minting and auction. Upon auction conclusion, the next machine seamlessly generates and begins the subsequent 24-hour cycle.
How are copyright (CC0) and original provenance defined for BCM NFTs?
All 101 chassis and mechanical frameworks are 100% original creations of our team, crafted in-house by our industrial designers. The entire 7,700 digital motorbike collection adopts the CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain) dedication, freely open for commercial application and remixing. Beverage logos appearing on bottle cap wheels remain the registered trademarks of their respective owners, forming a Pop Art cultural homage without commercial endorsement.
What is a "death spiral", and could it happen to BCM?
Death Spiral = The classic collapse loop in speculative GameFi: new player growth plateaus → reward token drops → veteran players liquidate → price drops further → terminal loop.

Historical precedent: Axie Infinity's SLP plunged 99%+ from peaks. Industry reports show 90%+ of GameFi projects have collapsed, with the bulk of $15B funding wiped out.

Why BCM is Different:
· BCM issues no speculative tokens, carrying zero token volatility risk
· BCM promises zero yield, eschewing mercenary speculators
· BCM value stems from design craftsmanship and tactile physical roots, rather than secondary-market manipulation.
How are the 7,700 BCM motorbikes composed?
Combining 101 bespoke chassis designs with 109 reclaimed bottle cap wheel elements, along with engines, headlamps, and handlebars, procedurally composed on-chain into 7,700 unique, immutable digital motorcycle assets.
What are gas fees, and how much will I pay?
Gas Fee = The transaction processing fee paid directly to network validators/miners, not collected by the BCM team.

When participating on Layer 2 networks: Gas costs are negligible (typically less than £0.20 / ~$0.25 per transaction).
I am a Web3 beginner, how do I participate?
No complex blockchain knowledge is required. When the daily mint goes live, simply:
1. Install a Web3 wallet (MetaMask / Rabby / Coinbase Wallet)
2. Fund with a modest amount of ETH
3. Visit bcm.motorcycle and connect your wallet to place a bid on today's release
4. Confirm the transaction, and the motorbike collectible will transfer directly into your wallet.
